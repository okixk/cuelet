What will be installed

@LOCAL_TEST_NOTICE@

Cuelet will be installed in Applications. Its audio driver will be installed for all users so that other apps can receive sound from Cuelet through Cuelet Virtual Microphone.

The installer can also update or repair an existing Cuelet installation. It never changes your Mac’s default input or output device.

Cuelet is ready to use immediately after installation. Restart your Mac when convenient to activate Cuelet Virtual Microphone; the installer does not restart Core Audio or force an immediate restart.
